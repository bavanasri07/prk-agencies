## Chosen Approach: Midnight Distribution Film

### Design Movement
Contemporary Indian corporate editorial with the restraint of premium FMCG packaging and the pacing of a documentary brand film.

### Core Principles
1. **Dependability over spectacle:** Every visual should feel physically plausible, operational, and believable.
2. **Editorial asymmetry:** Use offset compositions, long horizontal bands, and image-led sections rather than repetitive centered cards.
3. **Material richness:** Deep navy, wet reflective surfaces, soft grain, brushed silver, and warm amber details create depth without visual noise.
4. **Motion with purpose:** Transitions should suggest loading, routing, and delivery—never decorative bouncing or futuristic effects.

### Color Philosophy
Midnight navy is the anchor: it communicates trust, infrastructure, and evening logistics routes. Warm white creates a calm editorial counterpoint. Water blue appears only as a material cue in photographs and thin details, while amber marks action and human warmth. The signature brand color is **Route Amber `#D4A15E`**, used sparingly for CTAs, section numbers, and directional lines.

### Layout Paradigm
A cinematic single-page scroll built from alternating dark and warm-white scenes. Content enters from the edges like a film strip: a left-aligned hero, an offset introduction, a horizontal product runway, full-bleed location photography, and a split enquiry section. The layout favors wide gutters, tall image crops, and overlapping captions over a uniform grid.

### Signature Elements
- Thin amber route lines and numbered section markers.
- Micro-labels with spaced uppercase lettering, like logistics annotations.
- Wet-surface image treatment with soft navy overlays and restrained grain.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like a well-engineered system: direct, quiet, and responsive. Buttons shift a few pixels toward their arrows, cards lift subtly, images scale slowly, and the navigation becomes a glass control bar as the visitor moves deeper into the page.

### Animation
Use 400–900ms reveal sequences for major sections with opacity and translate transforms only. Stagger headings, body copy, and CTAs by 60–90ms. Product images scale from 1 to 1.04 on hover over 700ms. The brand strip moves slowly and pauses on hover. The process line reveals sequentially with a restrained amber draw effect. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by disabling nonessential movement.

### Typography System
Use **Sora** for architectural headings and navigation, with **Manrope** for readable body copy and labels. Headings use tight tracking, large uppercase lines, and deliberate line breaks. Supporting text stays compact at a comfortable reading width. Micro-labels use 0.18em letter spacing and a smaller size to evoke operational documentation.

### Brand Essence
**PRK Agencies is the Trichy-based distribution partner for businesses that need water, beverages, gas, and essential supplies to move reliably.**

Personality: **dependable, composed, connected**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are concise, confident, and location-aware. CTAs are practical and directional. Microcopy is specific rather than promotional.

Example headline: “The route behind every reliable supply.”

Example CTA: “Tell us what needs moving →”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a compact route-mark symbol: two parallel vertical strokes joined by a single offset amber crossbar, suggesting a road, a shelf, and a connection point. Pair it with a custom-spaced PRK AGENCIES wordmark in Sora SemiBold rather than a default text logo. The symbol should also work as a favicon.

### Signature Brand Color
**Route Amber `#D4A15E`** — a muted metallic amber that feels like late sun on a delivery route, distinct from generic gold and reserved for meaningful action.

## Style Decisions
- Use photorealistic commercial imagery, not illustrations or stock-style graphics.
- Do not invent company history, statistics, partners, authorization claims, or contact information.
- Keep conversion centered on a clear enquiry action, not e-commerce.
- Use placeholder contact labels without fabricated phone numbers, email addresses, or WhatsApp details.
